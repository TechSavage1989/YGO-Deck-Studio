YGO Deck Studio 3 v3.0.0-alpha.221-duelcore.1-rc261

Replay review. The replay feature was tested against full recorded duels on the real rules engine.

Verified working
- Playing a recorded duel from the start reproduces the same result and Life Points.
- Seeking to any decision, forwards or backwards, restores exactly the recorded turn, phase, Life Points and card counts in every zone; step forward and step back do the same.
- Save Replay and Open Replay round-trip through the saved file; a damaged file is reported as a replay divergence instead of playing on.
- A Checkpoint resumes live play at the saved decision with the recorded state.
- Rewinding a live duel and continuing from an earlier point works, and the resulting recording replays correctly.

Fixes
- After rewinding a live duel, an answer left over from before the rewind could be recorded into the new timeline; replays saved after that could stop later with "Replay divergence". Leftover answers are now discarded.
- When a replay reaches the end of the duel the status reads "Replay finished" instead of "Replay playing", the button shows Play instead of Pause, and pressing it replays from the start instead of doing nothing.
