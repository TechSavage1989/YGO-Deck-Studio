YGO Deck Studio 3 v3.0.0-alpha.221-duelcore.1-rc254

Battle, summon and effect sounds now use the sound effects the owner chose.

Sound
- Activation is the owner's magical whoosh; chain links use the shorter magical whoosh; targeting an incoming spell impact.
- Summons: Special (magic surprise), Fusion and Synchro (elemental spell casts), Xyz (magical rock spell), Link (spell casting), Ritual (earth spell with a spell cast).
- Attacks: sword slice for blade attacks, violent sword slice with a roar for beasts and dragons, energy beam blast for magic, fire spell impact for fire. Impact is a sword clash and block.
- LP damage is the epic spell impact; destroyed the medium explosion; negated the supernatural explosion; banished an outgoing spell impact.
- LP gain, stat changes and equip use the magic spell, magic burst, spellcast and sword slash recordings.
- Victory is the epic cinematic explosion; defeat the large underwater explosion.
- Each sound is trimmed to at most two seconds so the set fits in the application. Card handling, the Deck shuffle and the phase whoosh are unchanged.
