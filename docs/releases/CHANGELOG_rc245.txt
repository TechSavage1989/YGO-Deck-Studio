YGO Deck Studio 3 v3.0.0-alpha.221-duelcore.1-rc245

Test Duel presentation: events the Rules Engine reported but the app never showed.

- Coin tosses and die rolls now appear in the Duel Log, play a sound, and show a short on-board callout with the result.
- Counters placed on or removed from a card are logged, and cards on the field show a counter badge with their current total.
- Equips, control switches, Deck and Graveyard swaps, shuffled Set cards, unusable zones and Rock-Paper-Scissors results are logged.
- Missed timing is called out: when an optional "When... you can" effect cannot activate because it missed its timing, the log and an on-board callout say so. This is one of the most common questions when testing a deck.
- Damage calculation is logged for every battle with the final ATK or DEF values and which monster, if any, was destroyed.
- Log wording: movement lines no longer print "Position 5 Position" or "Position 10 Position". Positions are only mentioned on the field (Attack, Defense, Face-down Defense, or face-up and face-down for Spells and Traps). A face-down Spell or Trap being activated reads "is turned face-up".
- Activated-effect labels for Extra Deck monsters no longer start with the summoning-material line.
- Face-down opponent cards are never named by the new lines.

Tests: tests/test_rc245_event_presentation.py (new). Two engine-backed duels with a coin, dice, counter and equip list confirmed the lines against the real Rules Engine with zero rejections.
