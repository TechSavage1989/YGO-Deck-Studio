VALIDATION METHOD

The browser application (web/index.html, app.js, duel-core.js) was executed headlessly in Chromium 153 (JSPI available) against the real OCGCore engine:
- ocgcore-wasm 0.1.6 from npm served at /duel-engine/ocgcore, sql.js 1.13.0 at /duel-engine/sqljs
- Project Ignis cards.cdb (BabelCDB) at /duel-engine/data/cards.cdb and CardScripts (official/ plus base scripts) behind /api/duel-engine/script
- /api/cards served from cards.cdb converted to the app's card payload format
- IntegratedDuelController.start() called with a fixedSetup; the Player seat was driven by a scripted responder (passive, or the Dummy policy mirrored) so every decision on both sides was legal and automatic
- presentation delays and animations disabled; every engine message, Dummy decision and RETRY counted

Deck lists used (fictional sample lists built by card name): a vanilla beatdown list, Blue-Eyes, Salamangreat and Traptrix.
